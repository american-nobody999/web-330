Professor David's Discovery Notebook

Water freezes at 0°C (32°F) and boils at approximately 100°C (212°F) at standard atmospheric pressure.

Temperature reference points depend on conditions such as atmospheric pressure.

Laboratory Safety & Learning Notes
  • Enter measurements as numbers. Decimals are welcome!
  • Our laboratory uses standard conversion relationships.
  • Mass tells us how much matter an object contains; weight is the force of gravity on it.
  • Our U.S. customary liquid units are different from imperial units. Check your measuring tools!
  • Negative Kelvin temperatures are invalid for this introductory converter.
  • Results are educational and may be rounded to make them easier to read.
